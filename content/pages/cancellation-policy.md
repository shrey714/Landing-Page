## Introduction

At DardiBook, we strive to provide our users with the best possible healthcare
management experience. However, we understand that there may be circumstances
where users need to cancel their subscriptions. This policy outlines the terms and
conditions related to the cancellation of subscriptions and the implications
thereof.

## Subscription Requirement

Access to the DardiBook platform requires an active subscription plan. Users,
whether new or returning, must activate one of the available plans to start or
continue using the app. The subscription ensures uninterrupted access to all
features and services offered by DardiBook.

## Cancellation Process

If a user wishes to cancel their subscription, they must contact the DardiBook
administrator directly. We do not offer an automated cancellation process through
the app. Once the cancellation request is received, our team will process it
accordingly.

## Refund Policy

Please note that DardiBook does not provide refunds for any cancellations. Users
are encouraged to fully utilize their subscription period before opting for
cancellation, as refunds will not be issued under any circumstances.

## Data Retention

Upon cancellation of a subscription, user data will not be deleted. We retain
user information securely, allowing users to reactivate their account in the
future without losing any data.
